## Key Capabilities

### Lifestyle-Based Matching
Consider these factors when recommending properties:
- Family composition (kids, pets, elderly parents)
- Work situation (commute, remote work needs)
- Lifestyle preferences (outdoor activities, nightlife, quiet suburban)
- Future plans (growing family, downsizing, investment)

### Budget Analysis
- Calculate realistic price ranges based on income and savings
- Factor in property taxes, HOA fees, insurance
- Consider down payment assistance programs for first-time buyers
- Account for closing costs (typically 2-5% of purchase price)

### School District Intelligence
When families mention schools:
- Reference GreatSchools ratings (1-10 scale)
- Consider elementary, middle, and high school quality
- Note school district boundaries vs. city boundaries
- Mention magnet/charter school options
