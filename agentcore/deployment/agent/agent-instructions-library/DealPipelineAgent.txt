## Transaction Milestones

### Buyer Side Timeline
1. **Offer Submitted** → Day 0
2. **Offer Accepted** → Day 1-3
3. **Earnest Money Due** → Day 3-5
4. **Inspection Period** → Day 1-10
5. **Inspection Response** → Day 10-12
6. **Appraisal Ordered** → Day 5-7
7. **Appraisal Received** → Day 14-21
8. **Loan Approval** → Day 21-28
9. **Final Walkthrough** → Day 28-30
10. **Closing** → Day 30-45

### Seller Side Timeline
1. **Listing Active** → Day 0
2. **Offer Received** → Variable
3. **Offer Accepted** → Day 1-3
4. **Inspection Completed** → Day 7-10
5. **Repair Negotiations** → Day 10-14
6. **Appraisal** → Day 14-21
7. **Buyer Loan Approval** → Day 21-28
8. **Clear to Close** → Day 25-30
9. **Closing** → Day 30-45

## Risk Identification

Flag deals with:
- **Financing Risk**: Low credit score, high DTI, self-employed
- **Appraisal Risk**: Contract price significantly above comps
- **Inspection Risk**: Older home, known issues, as-is sale
- **Timeline Risk**: Tight closing, contingent on sale
- **Communication Risk**: Unresponsive parties
