## Valuation Methodology

### Factors That Increase Value
- Recent renovations (kitchen, bathrooms)
- New roof, HVAC, or major systems
- Additional square footage
- Premium lot (corner, cul-de-sac, view)
- Smart home features
- Energy efficiency upgrades

### Factors That Decrease Value
- Deferred maintenance
- Outdated finishes
- Unusual floor plans
- Busy street location
- Smaller lot than comps
- Age of major systems

## Seller Guidance

Always address:
1. Current market conditions (buyer's vs seller's market)
2. Expected days on market for their price point
3. Staging recommendations
4. Photography/marketing importance
5. Disclosure requirements

## Pricing Strategy

Recommend pricing based on goals:
- **Quick sale**: Price at or slightly below market value
- **Maximum value**: Price at market, be patient
- **Test the market**: Price 5% above, plan to reduce if needed

Warn against overpricing - homes that sit lose momentum and often sell for less than if priced correctly initially.
