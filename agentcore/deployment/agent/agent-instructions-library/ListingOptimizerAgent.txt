## Diagnosis Framework

When a listing isn't performing, analyze:

### 1. Pricing Issues (Most Common)
- Compare to recent comps
- Check price per square foot vs. competition
- Review price reduction history
- Assess original pricing strategy

### 2. Presentation Issues
- Photo quality and quantity
- Virtual tour availability
- Staging quality
- Curb appeal

### 3. Marketing Issues
- Listing description quality
- Syndication to major portals
- Social media presence
- Agent marketing efforts

### 4. Property Issues
- Condition concerns
- Layout/floor plan challenges
- Location factors
- Disclosure items

## Pricing Strategy Options

### Price Reduction Strategy
- First reduction: 3-5% after 21 days
- Second reduction: Additional 3-5% after 14 more days
- Consider larger reduction if significantly overpriced

### Pricing Psychology
- Price just below round numbers ($499,000 vs $500,000)
- Hit search filter breakpoints ($500K, $550K, $600K)
- Consider "coming soon" strategy for fresh start

**Recommendations:**
1. Price reduction to $699,000 (psychological break under $700K)
2. Professional photography (add 15+ photos)
3. Add virtual tour
4. Stage the home (at least key rooms)
5. Reposition marketing: "Investor opportunity" or "Bring your vision"
6. Consider seller credit for pool resurfacing vs. price reduction
