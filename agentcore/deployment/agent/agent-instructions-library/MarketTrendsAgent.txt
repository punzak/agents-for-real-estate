## Key Economic Indicators

### Interest Rates
- **30-Year Fixed Mortgage Rate**: Primary benchmark
- **Federal Funds Rate**: Fed policy indicator
- **10-Year Treasury Yield**: Mortgage rate predictor
- **Rate Spread**: Difference between mortgage and treasury rates

### Housing Market Indicators
- **Housing Starts**: New construction activity
- **Building Permits**: Future construction pipeline
- **Existing Home Sales**: Market velocity
- **Pending Home Sales**: Leading indicator
- **Case-Shiller Index**: Price trends

### Economic Health
- **Employment/Unemployment**: Job market strength
- **GDP Growth**: Overall economic health
- **Inflation (CPI)**: Purchasing power
- **Consumer Confidence**: Buyer sentiment
- **Wage Growth**: Affordability factor

## Buy Now vs. Wait Analysis

Framework for timing decisions:

### Buy Now If:
- You plan to stay 5+ years
- You found the right home
- Your financial situation is stable
- Rates are expected to stay flat or rise
- Inventory is low and competition is high

### Consider Waiting If:
- Rates are expected to drop significantly (1%+)
- Major life changes coming (job, family)
- Market is clearly overheated
- You're not financially ready
- Better inventory expected soon

### The Math of Waiting

If rates drop 1% but prices rise 5%:
- $400K home at 7% = $2,661/mo
- $420K home at 6% = $2,518/mo
- **Savings: $143/mo** (waiting wins)

If rates drop 0.5% but prices rise 5%:
- $400K home at 7% = $2,661/mo
- $420K home at 6.5% = $2,654/mo
- **Savings: $7/mo** (basically break-even)
