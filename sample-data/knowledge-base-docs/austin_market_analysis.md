# Austin TX Real Estate Market Analysis - March 2026

## Executive Summary

The Austin, TX real estate market in March 2026 shows balanced conditions overall, with significant variation by price point and neighborhood. After the rapid appreciation of 2021-2022 and the correction of 2023-2024, the market has stabilized with moderate growth.

### Key Market Indicators
- **Median Home Price:** $525,000
- **Average Price per Square Foot:** $285
- **Market Type:** Balanced (4.2 months inventory)
- **Average Days on Market:** 38 days
- **30-Year Mortgage Rate:** 6.45%
- **Year-over-Year Price Change:** +3.2%

## Interest Rate Impact Analysis

### Current Rate Environment (6.45%)

**Monthly Payment Examples (20% down, 30-year fixed):**
- $400,000 home → $2,020/month P&I → $2,920/month total (with taxes/insurance)
- $500,000 home → $2,525/month P&I → $3,625/month total
- $600,000 home → $3,030/month P&I → $4,330/month total
- $750,000 home → $3,788/month P&I → $5,288/month total

### Income Requirements (28% front-end DTI)
- $400,000 home requires ~$125,000 annual income
- $500,000 home requires ~$155,000 annual income
- $600,000 home requires ~$185,000 annual income
- $750,000 home requires ~$225,000 annual income

### Rate Sensitivity
If rates drop to 5.5%:
- $500,000 home payment drops from $3,625 to $3,340/month (-$285)
- Buying power increases approximately 8%

If rates rise to 7.5%:
- $500,000 home payment increases from $3,625 to $3,920/month (+$295)
- Buying power decreases approximately 8%

## Seasonal Trends

### Best Time to Buy
- **November - February:** Less competition, more negotiating power
- **Typical discount:** 2-4% below spring prices
- **Inventory:** Lower but less competition

### Best Time to Sell
- **March - May:** Peak buyer activity, highest prices
- **Typical premium:** 3-5% above winter prices
- **Multiple offers:** Most common during this period

### Current Season (March)
- Spring market is heating up
- Buyer activity increasing
- New listings coming to market
- Competition intensifying in desirable areas

## Investment Analysis

### Rental Market Overview
- **Average 2BR Rent:** $1,650/month
- **Average 3BR Rent:** $2,100/month
- **Vacancy Rate:** 5.2%
- **Year-over-Year Rent Change:** +2.8%

### Cap Rate by Area
- East Austin: 3.2% (appreciation play)
- South Austin: 4.1% (balanced)
- North Austin: 3.8% (tech employment driven)
- Suburban: 4.5-5.0% (cash flow focused)

### Investment Considerations
At current rates (6.45%), most Austin properties are negative cash flow investments. Investors should focus on:
1. Appreciation potential (East Austin, Mueller)
2. Value-add opportunities (fixer-uppers)
3. ADU potential for additional income
4. Long-term hold strategies

## Affordability Analysis

### First-Time Buyer Profile
**Assumptions:** $80,000 saved, $150,000 household income, good credit

**Maximum Purchase Price by Down Payment:**
- 20% down: $400,000 (no PMI, limited options)
- 10% down: $640,000 (PMI ~$250/month)
- 5% down: $605,000 (PMI ~$350/month)
- FHA 3.5%: $600,000 (MIP ~$400/month)

**Recommended Strategy:** 10% down at $550,000-$600,000 price point
- Preserves cash reserves
- Accesses better inventory
- PMI can be removed at 20% equity

### Best Neighborhoods for First-Time Buyers
1. **South Austin (78745):** Best schools for price, median $485K
2. **Mueller (78723):** Walkable, new construction, median $545K
3. **East Austin (78702):** Best appreciation, urban lifestyle, median $585K
