# Home Valuation Guide - Austin TX Market

## Overview
This guide provides methodology and data for accurate home valuations in the Austin, TX real estate market.

---

## Valuation Methodology

### Comparable Sales Approach (Primary Method)

The most reliable method for residential property valuation uses recent comparable sales (comps). Key criteria for selecting comps:

1. **Location:** Same neighborhood or zip code
2. **Recency:** Sold within last 90 days (prefer 30-60 days)
3. **Size:** Within 15-20% of subject property square footage
4. **Bedrooms/Bathrooms:** Similar configuration
5. **Age:** Within 10-15 years of subject property
6. **Condition:** Similar level of updates/renovation

### Adjustment Factors

**Size Adjustments:**
- Price per square foot varies by neighborhood
- Larger homes typically have lower $/sqft
- Adjustment: Use neighborhood average $/sqft

**Bedroom/Bathroom Adjustments:**
- Additional bedroom: +$15,000 to +$25,000
- Additional full bathroom: +$10,000 to +$20,000
- Additional half bathroom: +$5,000 to +$10,000

**Condition Adjustments:**
- Updated kitchen: +$25,000 to +$50,000
- Updated bathrooms: +$10,000 to +$25,000 each
- New roof: +$10,000 to +$20,000
- New HVAC: +$5,000 to +$10,000
- Original/dated condition: -10% to -15%

**Lot Size Adjustments:**
- Premium for larger lots: $5-15 per additional sqft
- Pool: +$20,000 to +$40,000 (varies by condition)
- Views: +5% to +15%

## Factors That Increase Home Value

### High-Impact Improvements
1. **Kitchen Renovation:** +$25,000 to +$50,000 (ROI: 70-80%)
2. **Bathroom Renovation:** +$10,000 to +$25,000 (ROI: 60-70%)
3. **New Roof:** +$10,000 to +$20,000 (ROI: 60-65%)
4. **Pool Addition:** +$20,000 to +$40,000 (ROI: 40-50%)

### Moderate-Impact Improvements
1. **New HVAC:** +$5,000 to +$10,000 (ROI: 50-60%)
2. **Hardwood Floors:** +$5,000 to +$15,000 (ROI: 70-80%)
3. **Fresh Paint:** +$2,000 to +$5,000 (ROI: 100%+)
4. **Landscaping:** +$3,000 to +$8,000 (ROI: 80-100%)

### Location Premiums
- Walk to Zilker Park: +5-8%
- Top-rated school zone: +8-12%
- Cul-de-sac location: +2-4%
- Hill country views: +10-20%
- Waterfront/lake access: +15-25%

---

## Factors That Decrease Home Value

### Condition Issues
- Dated kitchen: -10% to -15%
- Old roof (15+ years): -$10,000 to -$20,000
- Old HVAC (15+ years): -$5,000 to -$10,000
- Foundation issues: -15% to -25%
- Deferred maintenance: -5% to -10%

### Location Negatives
- Busy street: -3% to -5%
- Near commercial: -2% to -5%
- Power lines: -2% to -4%
- Flood zone: -5% to -15%
- Poor school district: -5% to -10%

### Layout Issues
- No primary bathroom: -5% to -8%
- No garage: -3% to -5%
- Unusual floor plan: -3% to -7%
- Small lot: -2% to -5%
